## Notes (hand-written)

- Spec and decisions so far: `design/dooblr-design-system.md` §17 dooblr-meter (MT).
- `dooblr-meter/README.md`
- Concept (pre-build): `~/Dropbox/grok_plugin_design_files/dooblr-meter-concept.md`.
- **Audio contract:** zero latency; the buffer is not touched (bit-exact) unless Listen or Mono is
  on. Auditions crossfade over 20 ms. Listen and Mono are not automatable and always load Off.
- **Threads:** the audio thread runs K-weighting, true peak and 100 ms slot sums and pushes slot
  records and short audio chunks through lock-free FIFOs; an analysis thread (~30 Hz) does
  gating, LRA, FFTs, translation and findings and publishes an immutable snapshot. No locks or
  allocation on the audio thread (tested).
- **Song mode** indexes slots by the host's `timeInSamples` while playing, so a replayed section
  overwrites itself. Live mode accumulates since Reset.
- **Parameters are append-only** (13 in 0.1.0, `stateVersion` 1). None are automatable: they
  are settings and auditions, not processing.
- **UI rules:** generic target names only (no service names or logos); no hint text on graphs;
  every control does something (a test changes each parameter and checks the effect). Above
  1080x660 the whole surface scales uniformly (up to 2x) instead of stretching the panels.
- **Deferred from 0.1:** reference-track comparison, report export, novelty-based section
  detection, hum and noise-floor checks. No controls are shown for them.
- **Known limits (0.1.0):** sections are fixed 4 s segments; a partial Song-mode replay that
  starts mid-segment adds to that segment's spectrum instead of replacing it; tables hold up to
  2 h; Live saves only the last 20 minutes; resonance detection works on 1/3-octave data;
  bright hi-hats can trip the sibilance check. The views repaint on a 30 Hz timer and draw paths
  in `paint()` (not the §3 pre-rendered / display-synced model yet).
