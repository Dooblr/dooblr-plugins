### High-pass (0.1.3)

- A 12 dB/oct Butterworth HPF on the main audio path, in front of the notch bank. Parameters
  `hpf` (bool, default **off**) and `hpfFreq` (20–400 Hz, log, default 80 Hz). Both are
  automatable APVTS parameters, listed last so older session indices do not move.
- **UI:** a dooblr pill (`ToggleButton`) labelled HPF and a Freq knob sit in the left column
  under Auto / Ring-out / SC, not under Advanced. The knob dims while the switch is off but
  stays live. The layout is tested at 940×600, 1080×660 and 2400×1400.
- **Off is bit-identical:** mix at 0 leaves the buffer untouched. Toggling crossfades 5 ms
  against the dry input; cutoff glides 20 ms and coefficients refresh every 16 samples.
- **Detector sees post-HPF audio.** The HPF runs first on the mic path, then the engine. The
  FFT detector listens to the processed output (or to the unfiltered sidechain when SC is
  on). Rumble on the mic therefore does not seed notches; a reference mic on the sidechain
  is left as the host sent it, because that is the room, not the channel being cut.
- **Graph:** while HPF is on, the plot shades the cutoff region in `accent` (a band from the
  left edge to the cutoff, plus the Butterworth cut hanging from the 0 dB baseline). No
  hint text.
